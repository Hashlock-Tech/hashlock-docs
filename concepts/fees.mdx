description: "Who pays the protocol fee, how much, when it becomes known, and what it costs on top in network fees."

## The protocol fee

- **The taker pays it.** The taker is whoever created the RFQ, public or private. A maker never pays it.
- **It is a share of what the taker gives**, in basis points, in that leg's own asset, and it is paid **on top** of the amount. Selling 1 BTC means funding 1 BTC plus the fee; the counterparty still receives exactly 1 BTC.
- **The rate is set per deployment.** Every swap records it: `swap.feeBps`, `swap.feeAmount` (base units), `swap.feeAssetId` and `swap.feePayerId` in the [`GET /v1/swaps/{id}`](/api-reference/introduction) response. The amount is `floor(amount × feeBps / 10000)`. Rounding is always down.

## When the amount is known

The fee is fixed when the swap is agreed, on the agreed amount of the taker's leg. Before that:

| The taker… | Fee known at RFQ creation? |
|---|---|
| sells (`sell_base`) | Yes — the fee is on `baseAmount`, which the RFQ fixes. |
| buys with a private order (`buy_base` + `askAmount`) | At the asked price. A counter-proposal changes the price, and the fee with it. |
| buys with a public RFQ (`buy_base`) | Only the rate. The amount follows the maker's quote. |

The web app and the Telegram Mini App show this line on the order form before the order is posted.

## How it is paid

On EVM, TRON and Solana the fee is paid **in the same transaction** as the leg; on Bitcoin, ideally in the same transaction. It goes straight to the protocol treasury and is never held in the escrow.

| Rail | How |
|---|---|
| EVM | `createSwap` transfers the fee to the treasury on top of the amount. An ERC-20 leg approves `amount + fee`; a native leg sends `amount + fee` as the value. |
| TRON | `fund` transfers the fee to the treasury. A TRC-20 leg approves `amount + fee`; a native TRX leg sends `amount + fee` as the call value. |
| Solana | The fund instruction transfers the fee to the treasury: to its token account for an SPL leg, in lamports for native SOL. |
| Bitcoin | `feeAmountSats` to `feePayTo`, ideally as a second output of the escrow payment, otherwise as a separate transfer **from the same wallet** (only confirmed payments count). The leg does not count as funded without it. |

The fee is part of the escrow's identity on EVM, TRON and Solana: an escrow funded with a different fee is a different escrow, and does not fund the swap.

<Warning>
**The fee is not refunded.** It is paid at funding, so a leg that is later refunded after its timelock returns the amount, not the fee.
</Warning>

## Small amounts

A Bitcoin fee below 546 sats — the smallest output nodes relay — cannot be paid, so it is **waived**: the swap records a fee of `0`. On EVM, TRON and Solana tokens any amount is paid, including fees of a single base unit.

## Network costs on top

These go to the network, not to Hashlock:

- **Gas / transaction fees** for each transaction you sign: funding (plus `approve` on EVM and TRON), and a claim or refund if you send it yourself. Where the keeper claims for you, it pays the claim's network fee.
- **TRON energy.** Each contract call — approve, fund, claim, refund — burns TRX for energy and bandwidth unless the account has staked them.
- **Solana rent.** Funding a Solana leg creates the escrow account (and, for an SPL leg, its token account). Their rent — about 0.0032 SOL for an SPL leg, about 0.0017 SOL for native SOL — is paid by the funder and is not returned: the program never closes an escrow, because a closed address could be initialised again on the same terms.
